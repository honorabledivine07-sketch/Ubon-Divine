AgroPolyBot is an award-winning engineering innovation that transforms cassava starch into environmentally friendly bioplastic, offering a sustainable alternative to conventional plastic materials. Developed with Kingdom Tech's Concept, the project combines engineering, environmental sustainability and innovation to address plastic pollution while promoting renewable bio-based materials.
