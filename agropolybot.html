Project Overview

AgroPolyBot is an innovative engineering project developed for the Battle of the Bots 2.0 competition. The project addresses two critical global challenges: plastic pollution and agricultural waste management.

By extracting starch from cassava and processing it into bioplastic, we created a sustainable, biodegradable material that can replace conventional plastics in various applications.

The Problem

  • Plastic pollution threatening ecosystems
  • Over 400 million tons of plastic produced yearly
  • Cassava waste from agricultural processing
  • Limited sustainable alternatives in Nigeria

The Solution

  • Extract cassava starch as raw material
  • Process into biodegradable bioplastic
  • 100% renewable and compostable
  • Utilizes local agricultural resources

Technical Process

1

Extraction

Cassava starch is extracted through washing, grating, and sedimentation processes.

2

Formulation

The starch is mixed with plasticizers and natural additives to create a flexible polymer.

3

Processing

The mixture is heated and molded into desired shapes and forms.

4

Testing

Quality tests ensure durability, biodegradability, and environmental safety.
