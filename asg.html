Project Overview

The Auto Switch Guard is a safety device that automatically disconnects electrical appliances from the mains when power is interrupted, and keeps them disconnected until manually reset after power restoration.

This prevents the dangerous situation where appliances left switched on during a power outage suddenly come back to life when electricity is restored, which can cause electrical fires, appliance damage, and even electrocution hazards.

The Problem

  • Electrical fires caused by unattended appliances after power restoration
  • Appliance damage from power surges when electricity returns
  • Electrocution risk from devices left switched on
  • Common problem in areas with frequent power outages

The Solution

  • Automatic cut-off when power is interrupted
  • Manual reset required after power restoration
  • Protection against voltage surges
  • Simple, affordable, and easy to install
