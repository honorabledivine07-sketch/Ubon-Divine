HelioAi Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HelioAi, a WhatsApp AI assistant, handles information when people communicate with it through WhatsApp.

1. Information We May Process

When you message HelioAi, the service may process your WhatsApp phone number, message content, message identifiers, and information required to receive and respond to your message. If you send media, additional information may be processed when the service supports it.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to receive messages, generate AI-assisted responses, deliver replies, maintain the service, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service from misuse.

3. WhatsApp and AI Service Providers

HelioAi uses WhatsApp and may use third-party automation and AI service providers to process messages and generate responses. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage and Retention

Information may be temporarily processed by the systems used to operate HelioAi. How long information is retained depends on the systems involved and their configuration. We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers supporting HelioAi or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are intended to be used to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You may stop using HelioAi at any time. To request deletion of information associated with your use of the service, contact the service operator using the contact details provided on the portfolio website linked below. Include your WhatsApp number with your request so it can be identified. Do not send passwords or security codes.

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal obligations and any limited information that must be retained.

See also our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Children's Privacy

HelioAi is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as HelioAi changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the HelioAi service operator through the Ubon Divine portfolio .
