Project Overview

HelioAI is an intelligent energy management system that uses IoT sensors and cloud computing to monitor solar panel performance in real-time. The platform tracks energy generation, consumption patterns, and battery health.

Built with ESP32 microcontrollers and cloud integration, HelioAI provides actionable insights through a user-friendly dashboard, helping homes and businesses maximize their solar energy efficiency and reduce electricity costs.

The Problem

  • Inefficient solar energy monitoring systems
  • Energy waste due to lack of real-time data
  • High electricity costs from poor energy management
  • Difficulty tracking solar panel health and performance

The Solution

  • IoT-based real-time solar monitoring system
  • Cloud dashboard for energy analytics
  • Automated alerts for maintenance and optimization
  • Smart energy consumption recommendations

Technical Process

1

Voltage, current, and temperature sensors installed on solar panels.
