Project Overview

SPAAR is a solar-powered agricultural robot that combines artificial intelligence with precision farming techniques. The robot autonomously navigates farmland, monitors crop health, detects diseases early, and optimizes irrigation.

Built on the ESP32 platform, SPAAR uses machine learning algorithms to analyze soil conditions, plant health, and environmental data in real-time, helping farmers make smarter decisions while reducing water waste and increasing yields.

The Problem

  • Manual farming is labor-intensive and inefficient
  • Excessive water waste in traditional irrigation
  • Late detection of crop diseases
  • Limited access to precision farming tools in Africa

The Solution

  • AI-powered robot for autonomous field monitoring
  • Real-time crop health analysis using sensors
  • Smart irrigation based on soil moisture data
  • Solar-powered for sustainable off-grid operation
