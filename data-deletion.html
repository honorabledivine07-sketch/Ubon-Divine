HelioAi Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 15, 2026

HelioAi is a WhatsApp AI assistant. You can request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the service by following the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Contact the HelioAi service operator through the Ubon Divine portfolio.
  2. State clearly that you want your personal information associated with HelioAi deleted.
  3. Provide the WhatsApp phone number you used to contact HelioAi so your request can be identified. Never provide your password or WhatsApp verification code.

What Happens Next?

Your request will be reviewed, and the information covered by the request will be deleted from systems under the operator's control where applicable, subject to legal obligations and legitimate security needs.

Information handled by WhatsApp or other third-party providers may also be subject to their own retention and deletion policies. We cannot promise deletion from systems we do not control.

Privacy Policy

Read the HelioAi Privacy Policy for more information about how information may be processed.
